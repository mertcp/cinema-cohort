# Cinema target groups (research notes)

Synthetic demo labels follow segments commonly used in industry reports — not made-up genre names.

| Demo target group | Industry basis |
|-------------------|----------------|
| **Frequent moviegoers (monthly+)** | MPAA THEME: attends once a month or more; small share of population, large ticket share. |
| **Occasional moviegoers** | MPAA: less than once a month, a few times a year. |
| **Infrequent / lapsed** | MPAA infrequent (~once per 12 months) + CRM “lapsed” win-back (Showtime, Filmgrail). |
| **Family moviegoing** | DCM “Main shoppers with children”; Fandango family motivators (matinee, bundles, holidays). |
| **Kids & tweens** | Same family research, split for programming: animation/matinee/holiday slots aimed at child decision-makers (with escorts). |
| **Young adults 16–34** | DCM core cinema audience; opening nights, social viewing. |
| **Horror & event fans** | DCM horror fandom (group size, opening weekend); treated as *community*, not “genre segment” only. |

References (public):

- [MPA THEME Report](https://www.motionpictures.org/) — frequency definitions.
- [DCM audience](https://www.dcm.co.uk/audience) — 16–34, families, horror fandom.
- [Showtime Analytics — behavioural segmentation](https://www.showtimeanalytics.com/news/behavioural-segmentation-strategy-for-cinemas)
